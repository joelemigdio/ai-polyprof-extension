# PolyProf 0.3.0 (alpha)

**Released:** 2026-10-19
**Supported pages:** The extension popup, and Cal Poly Student Center / Schedule Builder at `https://cmsweb.pscs.calpoly.edu/psc/*`.
**Data source:** Demonstration data — all ratings, review text, and review counts are invented.

## Summary

All three alpha features now work on the Poly Portal itself. Hovering a class shows every professor teaching it, a personalized summary comparing them sits beneath the list, and clicking a professor opens their ratings, tags, and reviews. The same information is browsable in the popup, and changing your preferences updates both surfaces immediately.

## What's new

- **Hover a class on the Poly Portal** → a card lists every professor teaching that section, ranked by how well they fit your preferences, each with both ratings and a fit line.
- **Personalized summary beneath the professor list** — compares the professors of that class against your saved learning styles and explains which one fits best and why.
- **Click a professor in the card** → their overall rating, similar-student rating, teaching-characteristic tags, and top reviews, with **Back to professors** to return.
- **Live preference updates** — change a preference in the popup and any open hover card re-renders without a page reload.
- Instructors with no rating match are listed as "no ratings available yet" instead of being hidden, so you can see the full roster for a class.

## Changed

- **Hover binds to the class row, not the instructor name.** Previously each instructor name was its own hover target showing one professor. Hovering a class now surfaces all of them, which is what the feature always described.
- **Demo ratings now appear on real Student Center pages.** Previously the content script showed real instructors only a "not available yet" card. See *Data and privacy* below — this is a deliberate alpha decision with a specific mitigation.
- The hover card was restyled to match the PolyProf popup, and its styles moved out of an inlined string into `src/content/hover-card.css`.
- Version bumped from 0.2.0; the four popup screens are unchanged since that build.

## How to use the extension

### Install the build

1. Run `npm install` once, then `npm run build`. The loadable extension is written to `dist/`.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` in Chrome.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (top-right).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select this project's `dist` directory.
5. Open Chrome's Extensions menu and pin **PolyProf** so the icon stays visible.
6. After pulling new code, re-run `npm run build` and click the reload icon on the extension's card in `chrome://extensions`.

### Set your preferences first

1. Click the **PolyProf** icon, then the gear in the top-right of the header.
2. Pick your **Department(s)** — this filters which classes appear in popup search.
3. Pick your **Class time(s)**. (Saved for future filtering; it does not affect results in this build.)
4. Pick your **Learning style(s)** — this is the one that drives all matching. Options are Project-heavy, Code evaluation, Labs, Clear grading, Structured, Detailed visual slides, Responsive to questions, and Light midterm weight.
5. The **Quick preferences** chips toggle the same learning styles, so the chips and the dropdown always agree.
6. Click **Save preferences**. They are stored in `chrome.storage.local` on this browser only.

### Use it on the Poly Portal

1. Go to Student Center or Schedule Builder and open a class search or your schedule.
2. **Hover over a class row** and wait a moment. A card appears listing every professor teaching that section.
3. Each professor shows an overall rating (cream star pill), a similar-student rating (dark green pill), and a fit line describing their teaching style.
4. Read the **Personalized summary** at the bottom of the card — it names the best fit for your preferences and flags any tradeoff.
5. **Click a professor** to see their tags and top reviews. Use **Back to professors** to return, or **Close** / `Esc` to dismiss.
6. The card is keyboard accessible: `Tab` to a class row to open it, `Esc` to close.

### Use it in the popup

1. On the Search screen, type a course code, title, or professor name, or leave it blank to browse.
2. Click a class card to see all professors for that course, ranked, with an **AI Recommendation** panel naming the top pick.
3. Click any professor, or **View Profile**, for their reviews.

### Watch the matching respond to your preferences

This is the main thing to exercise in this build:

1. Open a class hover card on the portal and note the order, percentages, and summary.
2. Open the popup, change your learning styles, and save.
3. The open hover card updates immediately — no page reload. The ordering, fit lines, and summary should all change.
4. With **no** learning style selected, match percentages disappear and the summary asks you to choose one. That is intended, not a bug.

## Known limitations

- **Nothing here is real data.** Professor names are real Cal Poly faculty, but every rating, review, and count attached to them is invented for UI development.
- **No AI model is called.** The summary is generated deterministically from your preferences and a fixed trait vocabulary, and the card says so. A genuine AI summary needs a server-side endpoint that does not exist yet, because a browser extension must never carry a provider key.
- **Only five professors have demo data.** Any other instructor shows "no ratings available yet". A class whose instructors are all unknown gives you a roster and nothing else.
- **Compare is disabled** in the popup. The button appears per the design but has no comparison view behind it.
- **Class time preferences are stored but unused** in matching.
- **Live portal compatibility is unverified.** The adapter is built against documented PeopleSoft structures and a local fixture, not a captured live page. If the card does not appear, work through the debugging checklist in [Student Center adapter assumptions](../STUDENT_CENTER_ADAPTER_ASSUMPTIONS.md).

## Data and privacy

- Preferences stay in `chrome.storage.local` on this browser; nothing syncs to an account or leaves the machine.
- No API keys or secrets ship in the extension bundle, and the extension makes no network requests.
- The content script is scoped to `cmsweb.pscs.calpoly.edu` and does not read, store, or transmit schedule records.
- **Demo ratings are shown for real instructors on the live portal.** Every card carries a "Demo data — invented ratings, not real PolyRatings" banner, and matching requires an exact, unambiguous name match. Do not distribute this build to students, and do not share screenshots externally without the banner visible. Before any student-facing release, either wire an authorized data source or restore the fixture-only guard described in the adapter assumptions doc.

## How to report problems

File an issue with: the surface (popup or portal hover card), your saved preferences, the class and professor involved, a screenshot, and what you expected instead. Do not include schedule records, student data, or authentication tokens.
